Pre-processes a CNF tree into an efficient evaluation structure.

Converts the entire CNF tree into a vector of clauses, where each clause is represented by a pair of sets: one for its positive literals and one for its negative literals. This is much faster to evaluate repeatedly than traversing the tree.
